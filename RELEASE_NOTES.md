The first public beta of WaveMini: Wave's mini player as its own tiny, native Windows app.

**Get it:** `WaveMini-Setup.exe` installs it and keeps it updated. `WaveMini.exe` is the same app without installing. Windows will say the publisher is unknown (the app isn't signed): choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's in it
- **Ad-free YouTube Music in one small window**, signed in or not.
- **Home and search in one tab**, with suggestions while you type.
- **Lyrics sung word by word**: each word fills in and lifts as it's sung.
- **Up next**: drag to reorder, play next, add to queue, radio.
- **Your library**: likes, history, playlists, albums and artists; save albums, make playlists, add and remove songs.
- **Floats on top**, snaps to a corner, tucks against the screen's edge.
- **Media keys** and Windows' media popup.
- **Small and light**: one file under 1 MB, drawn without loading a graphics driver, memory handed back when tucked away.

## Known gaps
- It's a beta: playback, sign-in and the installer have had little testing on real PCs.
- The search box has no cursor movement or text selection yet.
