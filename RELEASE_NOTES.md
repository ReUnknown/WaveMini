**Get it:** `WaveMini-Setup.exe` installs it and keeps it updated. `WaveMini.exe` is the same app without installing. Windows will say the publisher is unknown (the app isn't signed): choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New
- **Follows you across virtual desktops.** Switch desktops and WaveMini comes along. Turn it off in Account if you'd rather it stayed put.
- **A like you can see.** The thumb pops and a ring bursts out when you like a song.

## Smoother
- **Animations hold their pace.** Covers are now decoded off the window's own thread, the window no longer redraws through a full extra layer on every frame, and the glowing background costs a fraction of what it did while colors blend from one song to the next.

## Fixed
- **Shuffle and Radio on an artist's page** sometimes said the page couldn't be opened. They start the queue directly now.
