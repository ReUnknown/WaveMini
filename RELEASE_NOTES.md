**Get it:** `WaveMini-Setup.exe` installs it and keeps it updated. `WaveMini.exe` is the same app without installing. Windows will say the publisher is unknown (the app isn't signed): choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New
- **Picks up where you left off.** Open WaveMini again and your queue, the song you were on and how far into it you were are all there, paused and waiting, along with the pages you had open. If an update arrives in the middle of a song, the song carries straight on afterwards. It takes a few kilobytes on disk.
- **The chorus rides a wave.** Lines a song keeps coming back to get their own lyric animation: the words bob one after another in the song's color, glowing, with a soft light behind the line.

## Fixed
- **Following you across virtual desktops** worked once, then stopped: it came along to the second desktop but not back to the first. It now follows every time, including onto a desktop with no windows open.
